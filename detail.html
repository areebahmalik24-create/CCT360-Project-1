DIGITAL IDENTITY / ISSUE 01

The Life We Don't Post

What happens to the moments and memories we never share online?

WHAT REMAINS OFFLINE

We take pictures of birthdays, vacations, and fun moments with our friends. But there are also parts of our lives that we never share online, like staying home, cleaning our room, or spending time alone.

Social media can make it feel like everyone is always doing something exciting. But we only see a small part of their lives. Sometimes, the quiet moments we keep to ourselves are just as meaningful as the ones we share.
